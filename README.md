🛡️ Insurance Claims Analytics Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes insurance claims data to help insurance companies monitor claim trends, customer risk profiles, policy performance, and premium behavior. The dashboard provides actionable insights for claim management and business decision-making.

🎯 Business Objective

The primary objectives of this dashboard are:

Analyze claim frequency and severity.
Monitor premium amount trends.
Evaluate claim adjustments and policy performance.
Identify high-risk customer segments.
Compare claim behavior across regions.
Support data-driven insurance decisions.
